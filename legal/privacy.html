Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

DID Optimizer Pro ("the Service", "we", "us") — operated at dids.amdy.io — provides intelligent outbound caller-ID (DID) rotation, reputation monitoring, and analytics for call centers. This Privacy Policy explains what data we handle, how we use it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. Contact: privacy@amdy.io.

What we collect

  • Account data: name, work email, company name, and billing details.
  • Payment data: card details are collected for vaulting with our payment processor (PayPal). Full card numbers are transmitted to PayPal and are not retained by us; we store only the last four digits, card brand, expiry, and the PayPal vault token. If you use "Log in with PayPal", we receive the profile fields you authorize (such as name and email).
  • DID inventory & telemetry: the phone numbers (DIDs) you register, their usage counts, rotation history, and third-party reputation signals (e.g. spam-label status) for those numbers.
  • Call metadata: when your dialer requests a DID or reports a call result, we receive campaign ID, agent ID, the dialed number, call disposition, duration, and related technical data (SIP codes, timestamps). We do not receive or process call audio.
  • Operational logs: API requests, dashboard usage, and error reports.

What we do NOT collect or do

  • No call audio — the Service handles signaling metadata only.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not share telemetry between customer accounts.
  • We do not use your data for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How we use data

  • To provide the Service: select DIDs, track rotation, monitor number reputation, and return results to your dialer.
  • To run your account: billing (flat per-DID pricing), usage metering, invoices, and support.
  • To secure and improve the Service: detect abuse, debug, and measure quality in aggregate.
  • To communicate: transactional email (invoices, payment receipts, payment-failure and account notices) and, with consent, product updates.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where applicable we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the Service), legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, service improvement), consent (marketing email), and legal obligation (tax/accounting). You may withdraw consent at any time.

Controller / processor & data processing

For the personal data contained in the calls you place (such as the numbers you dial), you are the controller and we are your processor. We process that data only to provide the Service, keep it confidential, apply reasonable security, and use sub-processors only under equivalent obligations.

Retention

  • Call metadata and analytics: up to 365 days, then deleted or aggregated.
  • Account and billing records: for the life of the account plus the period required by tax/accounting law.
  • Payment vault tokens: until you remove the payment method or close your account.

Sharing & sub-processors

We share data only with service providers needed to run the Service: PayPal (payments), cloud infrastructure and database hosting, email delivery, and error monitoring. Each is bound to process data only on our instructions.

International transfers

Data may be processed in the United States and the European Union. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

Your California privacy rights (CCPA / CPRA)

California residents may request access to, deletion of, or a copy of their personal information, and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CPRA.

Your rights

You may access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, object to or restrict processing, and lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Email privacy@amdy.io and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes & contact

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above. Material changes will be announced by email. Questions: privacy@amdy.io.
